Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safe to use? ▼
Yes! Chrome Tamer uses official Windows APIs to manage memory. It doesn't modify Chrome or any system files. The worst that can happen is a background tab needs to reload when you switch to it.
Will my tabs lose their content? ▼
No. We only trim unused memory from background tabs. Your tab state, forms, and scroll position are preserved. Switching to a tamed tab may cause a brief reload, but nothing is lost.
Does it work with other browsers? ▼
Currently Chrome Tamer is optimized for Google Chrome. Edge, Brave, and other Chromium browsers may work but aren't officially supported yet. Firefox support is planned.
What's the difference between Free and Premium? ▼
Free mode tames tabs using 200MB+ of RAM. Premium drops this to 100MB and polls twice as fast (500ms vs 1s), meaning more aggressive memory savings. Premium also includes auto-start and lifetime updates.
