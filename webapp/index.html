Why Chrome Tamer?

Standard browsers are "greedy" players. They over-allocate resources to inactive tabs, assuming infinite memory availability. In reality, memory is a finite commons.

Chrome Tamer implements a Nash Equilibrium for your system. By treating every tab as a rational agent with a decaying utility score, we ensure that resources are only held by processes that provide active benefit. When system pressure rises, low-utility "players" are forced to deallocate, preserving the stability of the entire system.

🧠

Nash Equilibrium

Tabs bid for resources. Low-utility background tabs are pruned alphabetically or by domain redundancy when system pressure spikes.
